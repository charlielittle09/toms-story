How this works
One question at a time — the "Up Next" card is meant to be asked every few days, not all at once. Type an answer, record video or audio right on this page, or upload a photo or video from Tom's device — everything saves straight to the shared Google Drive folder.
  • Any question can be skipped — it stays in place, marked "skipped for now," and can be brought back anytime.
  • Questions are editable right on the card. Add your own with "+ Add a question," or remove one with the small ✕.
  • "Every Month" holds the two recurring prompts — each new entry adds to the list instead of replacing the last one.
  • If the camera/mic won't start on a device, "Upload photo or video" always works as a fallback — record with the phone's own camera app first, then upload the file here.
